Technology

Core Processes & Strict Quality Control

With strict process standards and precise testing methods, we forge superior product quality

01

Precision Time-Controlled Synthesis

Adopting a 4-8 hour optimal reaction cycle with precise temperature control to ensure complete reaction and stable crystal form, laying a solid foundation for subsequent purification.

02

Ten-Stage Gradient Deep Washing

Proprietary ten-stage gradient cooling washing process efficiently removes chloride and ammonium ion impurities, with purity indicators far exceeding national and industry standards.

03

Fully Enclosed Closed-Loop Production

From raw material feeding to finished product filling, the entire process uses sealed pipeline transport, isolating external contamination risks and ensuring product purity and batch consistency.
